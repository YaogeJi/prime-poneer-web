Together with Hongcheng Liu (University of Florida), I am co-organizing *[New Advances in Stochastic Optimization: Theories and Methodologies](https://submissions.mirasmart.com/InformsAnnual2026/Itinerary/EventDetail.aspx?evt=637)* at the 2026 INFORMS Annual Meeting.

The session is scheduled for Sunday, November 1, 2026, at Moscone Center South, Room 209 (Level 2), San Francisco.

The scheduled speakers are Baoyu Zhou (Arizona State University), Tianjiao Li (IBM Research), Grani Adiwena Hanasusanto (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign), Zhe "Jimmy" Zhang (Purdue University), and Anton Malandii (Brown University).
